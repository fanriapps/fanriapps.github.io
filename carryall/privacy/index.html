Privacy policy — Carryall

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Carryall has no account, no server and no analytics. Your documents stay on your device, encrypted, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.

The app is offline by design, so the copy most people read is the one inside it — the same text, in English, Russian, German and Spanish, available with no network at all. This page exists because a policy you can only read after installing is not a policy you can decide on.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-up, no user account and no server of ours to talk to. We receive no personal data from it, no usage statistics and no crash reports, because it contains no analytics or crash-reporting library at all.

What stays on your device

Everything you put in: documents and their scans, dates, travellers, trips and notes. They live in an encrypted vault inside the app's private storage, opened by the PIN you choose. Automatic system backup to Google Drive is switched off, so the platform does not copy the vault off the phone either.

Permissions

The camera, only while you photograph a document: the app never opens it on its own. Photos, only the picture you choose in the system picker. Notifications, only for the expiry reminders you switch on. Nothing is read in the background.

When the app uses the network

Once, and only if you ask: the button that downloads the document scanner asks Google Play services to fetch it. That request contains nothing from your vault. The app opens no connection of its own; the scanner and Play services are Google components, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Backups you make yourself

The export file is encrypted with a password only you know and is written to your device. It leaves the phone only when you hand it to another app from the share sheet, and where it goes after that is that app's business, not ours.

The problem report

The app keeps a small log on the device: events and error codes, never document contents, names, numbers or file names. Nothing is sent automatically. Settings → Help shows you the log in full, and sending it is one more deliberate step you can decline. You can erase it there too.

Children

The app is a document safe for general use and is not directed at children. Since it collects nothing, it collects nothing from anybody, at any age.

Changes to this policy

If it changes, the new version appears in the app with a new date at the top, and on this page. Anything that reduced what stays private would arrive as an app update you choose to install.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the app does with your data: carryall.app@gmail.com
